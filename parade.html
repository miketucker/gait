Humanoid Parade

Every simulated humanoid walking together: the test biped, the kid, ogre and giant, and the five comic bipeds, each playing back its own simulated stride at its own speed, drawn as cel-shaded spheres, tubes and cones.

    Each walker plays back the stride its simulation found (the same bone poses, stride period and speed as in the atlas), so the slow ones (the hood at 0.6 m/s, the ogre at 0.7, the pear at 0.8) drift back through the crowd. The bodies here are drawn around the skeleton from each body's outline; the dynamics are the atlas's. The track loops: a walker who passes the camera comes back out of the haze.
