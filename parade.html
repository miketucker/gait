Humanoid Parade

Every simulated humanoid walking together: the test biped, the kid, ogre and giant, and the five comic bipeds, each playing back its own simulated stride at its own speed, with bones, tendons and activated muscles visible.

Bones Tendons Muscle activation: low high

    Each walker uses the atlas's bone geometry, muscle attachment paths and muscle cross-sectional areas. Muscle color follows activation through its simulated stride; pale segments show tendons. Each plays back the same bone poses, stride period and speed as in the atlas, so the slow ones (the hood at 0.6 m/s, the ogre at 0.7, the pear at 0.8) drift back through the crowd. The track loops: a walker who passes the camera comes back out of the haze.
